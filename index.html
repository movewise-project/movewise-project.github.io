Problem & Solution

Moving is Guesswork, Furnishing is a Blind Experiment

Until now, room scanning, moving estimates, and interior design lived in isolated tools. Movewise connects the entire journey under one roof.

⚠️

The Moving Dilemma

  • ✕ A sofa fitting the old home gets stuck in the new entrance or narrow staircase.
  • ✕ Moving companies quote without seeing both homes, causing surprise delays.
  • ✕ Furniture is bought without accounting for walkways, door swings, and sockets.
  • ✕ Unplanned truck loading creates chaos and damage during destination unloading.
✨

The Movewise Solution

  • ✓ Step-by-step motion feasibility from source room, through stairs, into the truck, and onto destination placement.
  • ✓ Fast iPhone LiDAR scanning with uncertainty estimation and geometric motion planning.
  • ✓ Reverse unloading order (LIFO): items needed first are packed last.
  • ✓ Guided interior layout honoring clearances, fixed utilities, and catalog dimensions.
System Architecture

Seamless 4-Stage Move & Design Pipeline

From the mobile LiDAR scanning app to the cloud-powered 3D web planning panel.

1. 3D LiDAR Scanning

Rooms, doors, corridors, and stairs are captured using iPhone LiDAR. Inaccessible zones are added manually via floorplan input.

Room & Item Inventory
02
🚪

2. Bottleneck & Motion Feasibility

Solving the classic 'Piano Mover's Problem' to calculate clearance, tilting, rotation angles, and narrow bottleneck navigation.

Motion Simulation
03
🚛

3. Smart Truck Loading

Cargo volume is organized in reverse unloading sequence, computing crew size, estimated hours, and disassembly needs.

Logistics Optimization
04
🛋️

4. Verified Interior Design

Rooms in destination home are arranged according to interior guidelines: clearances, conversation zones, and plumbing constraints.

Innovation

Comparison with Existing Solutions

Movewise is the first platform that brings scanning, interior design, and home-to-home move planning under one roof.

Solution Strength Limitation Movewise Advantage
Apple RoomPlan & magicplan LiDAR room scanning and floor plan generation No passage feasibility or motion planning ✓ Connects the scan directly to physical motion analysis
IKEA Kreativ & Planner 5D Furniture visualization and automatic layout Never checks how items will reach the room or pass through doors ✓ Validates layouts against door, corridor and staircase constraints
Smart Moving & ItemFits Single-item fit and rotation checks for doors and elevators Dimensions entered by hand; no multi-item or route planning ✓ Solves chained bottlenecks across the whole home, plus item ordering
Yembo (AI Video) Moving quotes from inventory and volume estimates No route feasibility, layout or moving-order plan ✓ Computes truck loading order (LIFO) and assembly constraints
Value Proposition

Built For Every Stakeholder

Movewise eliminates friction and costly surprises for both consumers and logistics professionals.

🚚

Moving Companies

Quotes given without seeing both homes

Pre-move survey with crew size, truck space, disassembly requirements, and optimized loading order.

🏡

Home Movers

Anxiety over whether items will fit

One transparent plan proving what fits and how to move each piece without surprises.

🛋️

Home Furnishers

Layouts ignoring real spatial dimensions

Guided design taking into account clearances, door swings, and wall sockets.

Furniture Retailers

Pre-purchase path validation confirming the item can physically enter the customer's apartment.
